What is this?

YouTube Audio Downloader turns YouTube videos and playlists into MP3 audio files you can listen to offline, anywhere. It's free, needs no account, and runs straight from your browser.

How to use it

1

Copy the YouTube video or playlist URL.

2

Paste it into the box above.

3

Press Download and wait for the progress bar.

4

Your MP3 — or a ZIP for playlists — downloads automatically.

Format & quality

Every download is converted to MP3 at 192 kbps, with the cover art and title/artist metadata embedded into the file — ready for any music player.

Limits

Playlist size Up to 50 videos
File size Up to 250 MB per audio

Rate limit 10 per hour / 50 per day (when online)
Storage Files are removed after 1 hour

What it cannot do

  • Download video — this tool extracts audio (MP3) only.
  • Download from sites other than YouTube.
  • Download private, age-restricted, or region-locked videos.
  • Download broadcasts that are still live — wait for them to end.
  • Remove DRM or bypass copyright protection.

Only download videos you own or have permission to use.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes — completely free, with no account, sign-up, or watermark.

Does it download video or audio?

Audio only. Videos are converted to MP3, so no video file is saved.

Videos downloaded will be implemented in the future.

What quality are the downloads?

MP3 at 192 kbps, with cover art and title/artist metadata embedded.

How many videos can I download at once?

Playlists are limited to 50 videos, and up to 3 downloads run at the same time.

Can I download any YouTube video?

No. Only public videos work — private, age-restricted, and region-locked videos can't be downloaded.

Why can't I download a livestream?

Live broadcasts are rejected because only the already-aired portion exists. Wait until the stream ends, then download the complete archive.

How long are my files kept?

Files are removed automatically after about 1 hour, so download them right away.

Why do I see a rate limit?

Limits (10 per hour / 50 per day per IP) keep the service fair for everyone when it's online.
